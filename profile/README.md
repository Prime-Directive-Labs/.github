## What you can do

- **Build your Sentinel** — give it a persona, choose its tools, pick the model behind it (hosted frontier models, or your own local / fine-tuned model), and set the rules it runs under.
- **Delegate real work** — let it act on your behalf: research, draft, monitor, execute. One Sentinel is an assistant; several, coordinated, are a workforce.
- **Extend it** — plug in connections, plugins, and data from the marketplace. Soon: build and share your own Sentinels from your profile.

## You stay in control

Autonomy is only useful if you can bound it. Every Sentinel runs inside limits *you* set:

- **Guardrails you define** — the Guardian Protocol checks each action against your rules and blocks, warns, or flags.
- **A decision journal** — the reasoning behind every step, in plain language, so you can review it and correct it.
- **A human gate** — approval checkpoints for high-stakes actions.

We don't promise a Sentinel will never make a mistake. We make sure **you're never in the dark, and never out of control.**

## The bigger picture

Your personal Sentinel is one half of the platform. The other is the **Native Sentinel** — our first-party system that points the same capability at civilization-scale coordination, growing into a family of domain Sentinels across economics, space, food, health, and more. Coordinated by the AETERNOS protocol, with an optional, non-custodial on-chain layer on Base for verifiable trust and access — web3 where it adds trust, never where it adds friction.
